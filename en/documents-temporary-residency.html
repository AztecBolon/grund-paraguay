Temporary Residency: What Documents You Need

Residencia Temporal is a status that grants the right to live legally in Paraguay: it's issued for 2 years with the option to renew for the same term, and opens the path to permanent residency. It's the first step to legalization, common to any of the further programs; the single document package for it is set out in Art. 50 of Ley N° 6984/22 "De Migraciones".

Temp. residency Perm. residency Investor route Citizenship

The document package for temporary residency doesn't depend on the applicant's category — unlike permanent residency (see the "Permanent Residency" page), where a separate system of 12 categories has been in effect since 2026. You'll need:

  1. Passport — valid, with enough remaining validity to cover the whole process.
  2. Consular visa — not required for Russian citizens thanks to the visa-free regime in place since 2014 (more on the "Visa, Documents, and Entry" page).
  3. Birth certificate — apostilled, translated into Spanish by a sworn translator registered in Paraguay (Traductor Público matriculado).
  4. Marital status certificate / marriage certificate — if applicable.
  5. International criminal record certificate — apostilled, translated.
  6. A written undertaking to abide by Paraguay's constitution and laws — completed in-country, at DNM.
  7. A sworn statement of your profession and residential address in Paraguay.
  8. In-person appearance at DNM — mandatory, cannot be done remotely.
Not officially confirmedImmigration-service sources mention a requirement that the criminal record certificate be valid for 90 days from its issue date; the official text of the law doesn't set this out. We don't treat this as a final deadline and confirm the current requirements individually before filing.

The government fee under the general regime is ₲2,787,550 (cash) / ₲2,864,208 (card), in effect since July 1, 2025. The status is granted for 2 years with the option to renew for the same term, and is forfeited after a continuous absence from the country of more than 1 year.

Sources: Ley N° 6984/22 "De Migraciones" (Art. 50) — as summarized by Paraguayan law firms, without direct cross-checking against the law's text on the official portal; VF Paraguay Gestiones, Romero Machuca & Asociados. Confidence on the document list — medium-high; on the criminal record certificate's validity period — low-medium.

Next comes conversion to permanent residency. If you've already decided to move, request a consultation — we check your document package before filing, not after a rejection.
