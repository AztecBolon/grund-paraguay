Migration & Residency

DNM— Dirección Nacional de Migraciones
Paraguay's migration service. It issues residencies, keeps statistics, and sets applicant categories and fees (see DNM Resolution N° 407/2026 on the "Services" and "Process & Timeline" pages).

Mentioned on: Pricing, Temporary Residency — Documents, Services, Visa and Entry, Process & Timeline, Risks

Residencia Temporal— temporary residency
Granted under Ley N° 6984/22 "De Migraciones" (Art. 46), valid for up to 2 years with the option to renew for the same term, and forfeited if you're out of the country for more than 1 year.

Mentioned on: Case Studies, Confidentiality (client agreement), Services

Residencia Permanente— permanent residency
Granted for 10 years with the option to renew, and forfeited after more than 3 consecutive years outside the country. Via the standard route it becomes available after a full 2 years on Residencia Temporal; for investors, directly, without going through the Temporal (see SUACE below).

Mentioned on: Services

Cédula— local identity document
Issued already at the temporary-residency stage; its number is used as the RUC number (see below), and banks often require the Cédula itself to open an account.

Mentioned on: Case Studies, About Us, Citizenship — Documents, Services, Process & Timeline

Corte Suprema de Justicia— Paraguay's Supreme Court
It is the Supreme Court, not the migration service, that handles naturalization (citizenship) cases — the procedure requires the applicant's personal appearance and support from a lawyer.

Mentioned on: Confidentiality (client agreement), Services, Process & Timeline

Investment & Business

MIC— Ministerio de Industria y Comercio
Paraguay's Ministry of Industry and Commerce. It issues resolutions on the investor route (Resolución MIC N° 0283/2026) and administers the EAS portal.

Mentioned on: Services

REDIEX— Red de Inversiones y Exportaciones
Paraguay's state investment and export promotion agency under the Ministry of Industry and Commerce. It administers the preferential Maquila tax regime (see below) and the VUE electronic registration platform.
SUACE— Sistema Unificado de Apertura y Cierre de Empresas
The unified system for opening and closing companies. Company registration (EAS, S.A., S.R.L.) and the investor route to residency both run through it.

Mentioned on: Case Studies, Investor Path — Documents, Services, Process & Timeline

CIE— certificado de inversor
Issued as part of the investor route through SUACE. Per official figures, within 5 business days of submitting the complete document package.

Mentioned on: Case Studies, Investor Path — Documents, Services

EAS— Empresa por Acciones Simplificada
A simplified joint-stock company. The fastest way to register a company in Paraguay (officially, up to 72 business hours).

Mentioned on: Pricing, Services

S.A.— Sociedad Anónima
A joint-stock company. Registration takes 8–30 business days and requires a notarial deed and press publication.

Mentioned on: Pricing, Services

S.R.L.— Sociedad de Responsabilidad Limitada
A limited-liability company. Registration takes 15–30 business days.

Mentioned on: Pricing, Services

Régimen de Maquila— export manufacturing/services regime
A preferential tax regime (Ley N° 1064/97): a single 1% tax — on value added or on the invoice amount, whichever is greater — instead of the standard 10% IRE. Available to legal entities working under contract with a foreign company, including service exporters, not just manufacturing.

Mentioned on: Services

INDERT— Instituto Nacional de Desarrollo Rural y de la Tierra
The National Institute for Rural and Land Development. It administers agrarian-reform land — its status ("lote agrario") matters for due diligence on any real-estate deal, regardless of the buyer's nationality.

Mentioned on: Services

escribano— independent notary
Real-estate deals and company formation documents in Paraguay are formalized through this independent notary.

Mentioned on: Pricing, Services

Taxes & Filings

RUC— Registro Único de Contribuyentes
A single taxpayer ID number. Registered after obtaining residency; the number matches the Cédula number.

Mentioned on: Case Studies, Pricing, Citizenship — Documents, Services, Taxes for Foreigners, Process & Timeline

IVA— Impuesto al Valor Agregado
Paraguay's VAT. Rates are 10% (general) and 5% (reduced); filed via Formulario 120.

Mentioned on: Pricing, Taxes for Foreigners

IRE / IRE RESIMPLE— Impuesto a la Renta Empresarial
Corporate income tax. RESIMPLE is the simplified regime for microbusinesses with annual turnover up to ₲80 million: fixed quarterly payments of ₲60,000 to ₲240,000.

Mentioned on: Pricing, Taxes for Foreigners

IRP— Impuesto a la Renta Personal
Personal income tax. Rates of 8–10% on income from personal services above ₲80 million a year.

Mentioned on: Pricing, Taxes for Foreigners

Formulario 120— IVA tax return form
The exact filing frequency under the general (non-RESIMPLE) regime is confirmed individually — sources vary, and we confirm the current rule before starting work under the support subscription.

Mentioned on: Pricing

Marangatú— electronic tax filing system
The system run by Paraguay's tax authority (DNIT, successor to the former SET). For the RESIMPLE regime, it auto-generates a zero return if a filing isn't submitted on time.

Mentioned on: Pricing, Banking and Transfers

Health & Safety

IPS— state health insurance
Access is tied to formal employment and permanent-resident status; temporary residents don't get automatic access.

Mentioned on: Citizenship — Documents, Risks

carencias— waiting periods before coverage starts
The waiting periods before a policy starts covering a specific condition. At Asismed, one of the larger local insurers, it's 6 months for declared chronic conditions and at least 12 months for cancer, kidney, and liver disease; undeclared conditions aren't covered at all. More on the "Healthcare in Paraguay" page and in the "Medical Support" service.
medicina prepaga— prepaid medical plan (Spanish-market term)
The Spanish-speaking market's term for a prepaid medical plan — a recurring fee for access to a clinic network, rather than reimbursement against receipts. In Paraguay it refers to plans from local insurers like MAPFRE or Asismed — see the "Healthcare in Paraguay" page.
Declaración Jurada de Salud— mandatory sworn health declaration
The mandatory sworn health declaration you fill out when signing up for a policy with a local insurer (e.g. Asismed). What's declared in it determines the waiting periods and the scope of coverage — see the "Healthcare in Paraguay" page.
PAHO— Pan American Health Organization
The Pan American Health Organization, WHO's regional office. The source of the dengue statistics we cite on the "Risks & Difficulties" page.

Mentioned on: Risks

OSAC— Overseas Security Advisory Council
A U.S. State Department body for the security of overseas missions. It publishes threat-level assessments for foreigners by country, including Paraguay.

Mentioned on: Risks

Documents & Procedures

Apostille
A simplified international certification of an official document for use abroad — it replaces consular legalization for countries party to the 1961 Hague Convention (Russia since 1992, Paraguay since 2014). Criminal record certificates and other residency documents are apostilled in the country that issued them — more on the "Visa, Documents, and Entry" page and in the "Documents by Program" section.

Mentioned on: Temporary Residency — Documents, Permanent Residency — Documents

Traductor Público matriculado— sworn/certified translator registered in Paraguay
A sworn translator registered in Paraguay — not just any translator, but a specialist with local registration. Apostilled documents not in Spanish are accepted by the migration service and the courts only if translated by this kind of translator.

Mentioned on: Temporary Residency — Documents

MEC— Ministerio de Educación y Ciencias
Paraguay's Ministry of Education and Science. Under the system of 12 permanent-residency applicant categories (see DNM and "Permanent Residency"), a degree or qualification submitted as proof of solvency under the "professional" and "technician" categories must be registered specifically with MEC.
Resolución DNM N° 407/2026
The migration service (DNM) resolution in effect for cases filed since July 6, 2026 — it introduces a single system of 12 permanent-residency applicant categories, each with its own document requirements (employee, self-employed, retiree, dependent, and others). The full table of categories and documents is on the "Permanent Residency: Documents" page.

Mentioned on: Permanent Residency — Documents

Carta de Naturalización
The official document by which Paraguay's Supreme Court (Corte Suprema de Justicia) certifies that a foreigner has been granted Paraguayan citizenship. Not to be confused with residency — it's processed separately, not by the migration service. The document list is on the "Citizenship: Documents" page.

Mentioned on: Citizenship — Documents

Investor Pass
The name of the investor route to permanent residency without going through the temporary stage — the scheme runs under Resolución MIC N° 0283/2026, is administered through SUACE, and the official investor certificate is called CIE. The document list for the certificate is on the "Investor Route: Documents" page.

Mentioned on: Investor Path — Documents
