Mercado 4

Asunción's largest public market — not a tourist attraction but a place to try real Paraguayan food, including chipa.

PhotoWe don't have our own photos yet — see a selection for "Mercado 4 Asunción Paraguay" on Google Images →

Mercado 4 is Asunción's largest market, lively and loud, with no tourist-oriented polish. It's where traditional Paraguayan food is cooked and sold, above all chipa — cheese bread made with cassava flour, one of the country's leading food symbols.

It's not a place for souvenirs in the usual sense, but a way to see the city's everyday life — and a decent test of how ready you are for an un-touristy, dense Paraguay, if a move is being seriously considered.
