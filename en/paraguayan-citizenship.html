Paraguayan Citizenship

Paraguayan citizenship grants a passport with visa-free access to roughly 145 countries (Henley Passport Index) and a more stable legal status than residency.

This is a short reference page: we don't offer citizenship as a separately sold service. Here are the key facts for anyone who plans to go on from residency to a passport.

When you can apply

In our experience, you can apply for naturalization after 3–5 years if you hold a Paraguayan Cédula (legal basis: Art. 148 of Paraguay's Constitution). "Citizenship in a year," as some agencies promise, doesn't exist.

An exam in Spanish

When applying for citizenship you have to pass an exam on the fundamentals of the state — in Spanish. This requirement is in force.

Who handles the case

The naturalization case is handled by the Supreme Court (Corte Suprema de Justicia), not the migration service; the procedure requires appearing in person and legal representation.

Cost

Government fee: ₲43,051 — a state charge (source: Poder Judicial). Full support with a lawyer (lawyer, charges, etc., excluding the government fee) costs roughly from 5,000 USD; the exact amount is confirmed individually.
