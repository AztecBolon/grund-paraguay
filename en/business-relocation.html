Relocating a business to Paraguay

Relocating an organization isn't a single procedure — it's a package of several already-established services, put together for a company's needs rather than an individual's: registering the legal entity, the preferential Maquila tax regime where it applies, residency for relocating employees, and opening a corporate bank account. There's no separate government procedure for "company relocation" in Paraguay — we assemble it from mechanisms already described separately on this site.

The difference from ordinary company formation is one of scale and sequencing: instead of a single founder and a single residency case, there are usually several employees who need legal status at the same time as a functioning legal entity and account. We run this as one project, not as several independent orders.

What the package includes

  • Registering the legal entity — EAS, S.A., or S.R.L., the same "Company formation" service (see the card on the "Services" page).
  • Registering under the Maquila regime — if the business is manufacturing or exportable services (more below).
  • Residency for the team — a package of several cases under the already-existing "Residency" service, one per relocating employee.
  • Opening a corporate bank account — accounting for the requirements banks specifically apply to legal entities (see the section below).
  • Ongoing compliance — the same logic as for individuals, but at the company level: the legal entity's RUC and regular filings.

The Maquila regime — likely the main advantage for service companies

An official regime (Ley N° 1064/97), administered through REDIEX and the MIC: a single 1% tax — on either the value added created in Paraguay or the invoice amount, whichever is greater — instead of the standard 10% IRE. Any legal entity legally registered in Paraguay and working under contract with a foreign company qualifies; benefits include suspension of duties on imported raw materials and equipment, exemption from customs and port fees, and VAT refunds. The regime is explicitly open to more than manufacturing: per an official MIC document, current operators under the regime include a separate "Exportadores de Servicios" (service exporters) category — meaning it also fits relocating an IT or outsourcing company, not just a factory.

What isn't confirmedThe specific procedure and timeline for obtaining Maquila status, separate from the company registration itself, hasn't been researched as of this writing; before quoting an exact timeline and checklist, we confirm this individually through REDIEX for your specific case.

The company as a legal entity

Registration timelines are the same as for ordinary company formation: EAS through SUACE — up to 72 business hours with a standard charter, S.A. — 8–30 business days, S.R.L. — 15–30 business days. For an EAS, the legal representative must hold Paraguayan citizenship or a Paraguayan Cédula "with no exceptions" — in a relocation where no employee yet has residency, a proxy representative takes on this role at the start, until someone on the team obtains a Cédula.

Corporate bank account

The company needs a fully formalized corporate structure; physical presence to open the account is mandatory — doing it remotely is practically impossible. As a relatively new legal form, an EAS sometimes raises additional questions for banks and requires more careful support when filing.

Nothing in the sources we gathered directly confirms — or rules out — banks applying additional requirements specifically to capital or owners of Russian origin. That doesn't mean such complications are guaranteed not to happen, only that we found no direct confirmation of them. We confirm this specifically for your case before starting work, not after the fact.

Residency for employees — no separate "corporate visa"

There's no special visa in Paraguay for relocating employees or digital nomads: the whole flow runs through the same mechanism already described on the "Residency" page — Residencia Temporal under the general migration law, where remote work and a contract with the foreign (in this case, relocating) employer are part of the document package proving income source. There's no officially set minimum income threshold — the migration service assesses solvency from bank statements and contracts. Review takes 60–90 days. In practice, this means "relocating the team" isn't one procedure but several parallel residency cases, just with corporate documents (contract, company statements) instead of some of the personal ones.

Cost

The package is built from already-existing services — company formation, residency for each employee, and the compliance subscription — plus a separate Maquila component where needed. We calculate the final cost and package composition individually: it depends on the legal form chosen, the number of relocating employees, and whether the Maquila regime is needed.
