How We Work

Two sections that together answer the question «what to actually expect from working with us»: a step-by-step roadmap with honest timelines, and a direct conversation about the risks and difficulties people relocating actually face — without alarmism, but without hiding anything either.

Process & Timeline

A step-by-step roadmap from filing documents for temporary residency to citizenship: what happens at each stage, how long it actually takes, and when the window opens for the next step. The key figure is 7–8 years from moving to getting a passport, according to the director of the migration agency (DNM) — not "one year," as sometimes promised on the market.

Risks, Honestly

Climate and health, border areas with higher crime rates, the current regulatory window, and fraud schemes in the relocation market — what's worth knowing in advance, without sugarcoating and without alarmism. It's a rare page of its kind: most agencies simply stay silent about the difficulties of relocating.
