Public offer for the provision of consulting services

Draft — do not publish without legal review This document was prepared as a working draft for launching the first version of the site and has not yet been reviewed by a lawyer. Before a public launch it needs to be shown to a lawyer (ideally one practicing in Paraguay and familiar with the law applicable to the site), and the company's placeholder details need to be replaced with real ones.

This applies to the services described on the "Services" page: residency, ongoing support and compliance, citizenship, the investor route, real-estate due diligence, company formation, and support for business purchase/sale deals. It does not replace the individual agreement with the client, which fixes the scope and cost of a specific service (see the "About Us" page).

1. General provisions

1.1. This document is a public offer from Fortuna Vida E.A.S. (the "Provider") addressed to any legally capable individual or legal entity (the "Client") to enter into an agreement for consulting and support services related to relocation, residency, citizenship, business activity, and real-estate transactions in the Republic of Paraguay, on the terms set out below.

1.2. Acceptance of this offer occurs by submitting a request via the "Contact" page and subsequently signing an individual agreement with the Provider — the request by itself does not create obligations for either party until the agreement is signed.

2. Subject of the agreement

2.1. The Provider agrees to provide the Client with services within the scope agreed by the parties in the individual agreement, and the Client agrees to pay for these services on the terms of the agreement.

2.2. Government fees, taxes, and other mandatory payments to Paraguayan state authorities, as well as the services of third-party partners (for example, local lawyers and notaries), are not included in the Provider's service fees and are paid by the Client separately — see the "Costs and Rates" page.

3. Cost and payment terms

3.1. The cost of services is fixed in the individual agreement before work begins and is not changed unilaterally, except where the scope of work changes substantially and is separately agreed by the parties.

3.2. The full amount for the agreed service is due by the time the Cédula (CI) application is filed; details of the payment schedule are set out in the individual agreement.

3.3. Special requests from the Client (for example, finding housing or renting a car) are always possible; there is no fixed price list for them, since the effort can't be estimated precisely in advance, so they are billed at an hourly rate or as a package.

4. Rights and obligations of the parties

4.1. The Provider agrees to act in good faith, to inform the Client of changes in applicable Paraguayan law that affect the service being provided, and not to make timeline promises it knows to be inaccurate (see the Provider's position on the "Risks & Difficulties" page).

4.2. The Client agrees to provide accurate documents and information needed to render the service, and to promptly notify the Provider of changes affecting the progress of the case.

5. Liability of the parties

5.1. The Provider is not liable for decisions made by Paraguayan state authorities (including denial of residency or citizenship) where these do not result from the Provider's improper performance of its obligations.

5.2. Other liability terms, including the limitation and allocation of risk, are set out in the individual agreement.

6. Confidentiality

6.1. The handling of the Client's personal, financial, and other data in connection with the provision of services is governed by a separate confidentiality agreement attached to the individual agreement.

7. Force majeure

7.1. The parties are released from liability for failure to perform obligations where this is caused by force-majeure circumstances — extraordinary events the parties could neither foresee nor prevent, for example: a bank going bankrupt, a plane crash, or a tornado destroying the office. This also covers substantial changes to Paraguayan immigration law occurring after the agreement is concluded.

8. Dispute resolution

8.1. Disputes are heard in Encarnación (Paraguay), where the Provider is based. The governing law and other details of the dispute-resolution procedure are set out in the individual agreement.
