Paraguayan Citizenship

Paraguayan citizenship grants a passport with visa-free access to roughly 145 countries (Henley Passport Index) and a more stable legal status than residency.

Naturalization isn't an alternative to residency — it's its logical continuation for those who want a more stable status than Residencia Permanente and are willing to wait a few more years for it.

When it becomes possible

Naturalization is only possible 3 years after getting Residencia Permanente (Art. 148 of Paraguay's Constitution) — meaning the realistic full timeline from moving to holding a passport works out to 7–8 years, not "a year" as some agencies promise.

The path from moving to a passport

A timeline built from already-published ranges: Temporal (up to 2 years) → Permanente (usually 2–5 years) → a mandatory 3-year wait before applying for naturalization.

Who handles the case

The naturalization case is handled by the Supreme Court (Corte Suprema de Justicia), not the migration service; the procedure requires appearing in person and legal representation — it's a separate process with its own timeline, not an automatic continuation of the residency case.
