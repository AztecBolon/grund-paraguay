Buying & Selling a Business

Buying & selling a business is deal support for those who'd rather step into a running company or exit their own, instead of registering one from scratch.

A market with no standard statistics

The market for buying and selling businesses in Paraguay is small and opaque: major international deal trackers (e.g., TTR Data) don't cover Paraguay at all, unlike neighboring Argentina and Uruguay. That means there's no standard statistics on valuation multiples or deal volume, and few verified offers on the open market.

How we work without a ready-made benchmark

Since there's no market statistic to lean on, we support each deal individually — across three stages:

  • Independent valuation — based on the specific company's actual numbers, not a market multiple that simply doesn't exist for Paraguay.
  • Offer verification — due diligence on documents, liabilities, and the company's real state of affairs, not just what the seller says.
  • Handover support — the legal formalization of the business passing from one owner to another.
