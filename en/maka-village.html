Aldea Maká

A settlement of the indigenous Maká people near Asunción — a look at community life and craft goods bought directly from residents.

PhotoWe don't have our own photos yet — see a selection for "Aldea Maká Paraguay" on Google Images →

Aldea Maká is a settlement of the indigenous Maká people on the outskirts of Asunción, where tours are organized. Visitors can see traditional crafts made by residents and buy items directly, without intermediaries.

One caveat is worth making: in the material gathered here, this is the only source on the topic, and the question of how respectful this kind of tour format actually is toward the community itself, rather than turning it into a backdrop for tourists, is worth checking separately before recommending the route as an unambiguously positive experience.
