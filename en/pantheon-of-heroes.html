Panteón Nacional de los Héroes

The mausoleum of Paraguay's national heroes in downtown Asunción, with a dome in the spirit of the Paris Les Invalides — free admission and one of the capital's most symbolic sites.

PhotoWe don't have our own photos yet — see a selection for "Panteón Nacional de los Héroes Asunción" on Google Images →

Panteón Nacional de los Héroes opened in 1936 and is architecturally modeled on the Paris Les Invalides — the same massive neoclassical dome, the same solemn scale. It is the official mausoleum of Paraguay: key figures of Paraguayan history are buried here, including those who died in the Paraguayan (Triple Alliance) War and the Chaco War.

Admission is free, and the pantheon is open daily from early morning. An honor guard usually stands at the entrance — the scheduled changing of the guard is a small spectacle in itself for anyone who happens to be nearby at the right time.
