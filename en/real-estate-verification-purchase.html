Real Estate Verification & Purchase

Real estate verification & purchase is a due-diligence service on a specific property, protecting you from overpaying or from hidden risks in the deal. We don't run a listings catalog: the specific deal is checked by our partner on the ground, and we coordinate the process.

The outcome isn't a list of "good deals" — it's a report on one specific property: whether it's safe to buy and what to check before you sign.

What the partner checks

  • The chain of title documents — who owned the property and on what basis, before the seller.
  • The land's status — in particular, the risk of it being classed as "lote agrario" (inalienable land reform land under INDERT), which can't be freely sold to a foreigner.
  • Working through an independent notary (escribano) — not whoever the seller suggests.
  • A remote power of attorney — for those who haven't moved yet and can't attend the closing in person.

Region

On the ground we work in Encarnación and the Itapúa department. For properties in other regions of Paraguay, verification is only possible through remote contacts — we say so before work begins.

A cost that's easy to overlook: the annual ownership tax

Impuesto InmobiliarioAn annual property tax — 1% of the fiscal (assessed, not market) value of the property, which is re-indexed every year to inflation. For 2026, the fiscal value was indexed up by 4.1%. The payment deadline depends on the municipality (in Asunción, by March 31). This is an ongoing ownership cost, not just a transaction cost — worth budgeting for upfront, not discovering later.

One-off transaction costs

Beyond the annual ownership tax, the purchase itself carries separate one-off costs — notary fees, a registration fee, a municipal transfer tax, and VAT on the transfer if the seller is a VAT payer. The detailed cost breakdown is on the "Pricing" page.

Cost of the verification

The due-diligence fee is fixed per report, regardless of whether the deal closes; the exact amount is on the "Pricing" page.
