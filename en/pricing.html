Costs and rates: the government fee, separate from the agency's fee

Two separate blocks: the government fees and taxes you pay directly to Paraguay's authorities (which don't depend on us), and the cost of our services (full list on the "Services" page). We deliberately never blend these into one number — unlike some advertising on this market, where the government fee and the agency's fee are presented as a single "turnkey price."

Government fees and taxes (current as of the payment date)

Important caveatSome fee amounts reflect data as of July 1, 2025. As of July 1, 2026, migration service (DNM) fees were revised again (Decreto N° 6225/2026 and Resolución DNM N° 478/2026; the new calculation base is the minimum wage of ₲3,044,000, which rose 5% by July 2026), but as of August 19, 2026, the exact new amounts in guaraníes still weren't published in open sources — per DNM's own news releases, an official fee table exists on the migration service's website, but we couldn't access it directly at the time of checking. Fees are revised annually on July 1 along with the minimum wage — so we confirm the exact current amount for your payment date individually, rather than publish a figure that could be outdated within weeks. The current guaraní exchange rate against the dollar and the ruble is on the "Terminology" page.

Taxes after obtaining residency and registering an RUC: IVA (VAT) — 10% general rate, 5% reduced rate, filed via Formulario 120 (filing frequency under the general regime is confirmed individually — sources vary, and the exact rule requires checking against the text of Ley 6380/19 before it's shared with a client); the simplified microbusiness regime IRE RESIMPLE — for annual turnover up to ₲80 million, fixed quarterly payments from ₲60,000 to ₲240,000, with the Marangatú system auto-generating a zero return if none is filed on time; IRP on personal-services income — 8–10% for income from ₲80 million a year; late-payment penalty — from 4% (up to 1 month late) to 14% (over 5 months late).

Gap in sourcesThe exact current penalties for failing to file under the general IVA/IRP regime (not RESIMPLE) were not found in open sources — these are confirmed individually as part of the ongoing support subscription (see the "Services" page).

Example cost structure for a real-estate transaction (not a rate for a specific property): notary (escribano) fee — roughly 0.75% of the transaction amount for properties over ₲200 million (1–2% for cheaper ones), plus 10% VAT on that fee; registration fee — around 0.8%; municipal transfer tax — around 0.3%; VAT on the transfer itself, if the seller is a VAT payer — 5% of the presumed tax base (30% of the sale price), i.e., roughly 1.5% of the full property price. Total transaction costs in this example — around 2.3%, excluding VAT on the transfer.

Company formation costs: a simplified joint-stock company (EAS) — per estimates from industry business media, around 800 USD excluding government fees for a fully online registration; S.A. and S.R.L. — roughly 2,000–2,500 USD, including the notarial deed (for an S.A., also the mandatory press publication). No official state fee schedule by line item was found in open access — the amounts given are independent estimates from business media, not an official regulator price list.

Thresholds and amounts that our source material explicitly flags as officially unconfirmed (in particular, the specific financial requirements for the 12 applicant categories under DNM Resolution N° 407/2026 — see also "Process & Timeline") we do not publish as figures — they are calculated and confirmed individually against DNM's current rates and requirements at the time of your application.
