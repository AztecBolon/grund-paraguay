How Much It Costs to Live in Paraguay

Approximate monthly expenses in Asunción — rent, groceries, and utilities based on Numbeo and Expatistan data, with an honest caveat about how accurate these sources are.

Methodological note Numbeo and Expatistan, which this page draws on, aren't official statistics — they're crowdsourced data with a small sample for Asunción: users enter the prices they see themselves. Treat the figures below as a budgeting guide, not an exact price list.

Per Numbeo (snapshot from early August 2026, 57 contributors): renting a one-bedroom apartment in central Asunción runs around 3.65 million guaraníes a month, and outside the center, around 2.21 million; one person's expenses excluding rent run around $599 a month, and a family of four's around $2,242.

Per Expatistan: a family of four's monthly expenses run around 15.9 million guaraníes, and one person's around 7.8 million; the service itself notes its estimates are based on "a small amount of data." By the same source, Asunción is roughly 16% cheaper than Buenos Aires.

We couldn't find official government statistics on the cost of the basic consumer basket (from Paraguay's Central Bank or the national statistics agency) in the open web — such data would be more reliable than Numbeo and Expatistan.

It's important not to confuse this page with Pricing on the site — that page covers the price of our services and DNM government fees, while this one covers everyday living expenses in the country — two different kinds of "cost." Figures in guaraníes are easiest to check against the current exchange rate on the Terminology page.
