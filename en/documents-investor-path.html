Investor Route: Documents for the Investor Certificate

Direct access to permanent residency without going through the temporary stage — from 70,000 USD in investment. The document list for the investor certificate (CIE) below comes from the official SUACE portal PDF, not a summary of secondary sources.

Documents for the investor certificate (CIE)

  1. A notary-certified copy of your ID document — Cédula from your home country or passport.
  2. A criminal record certificate from your home country — apostilled or legalized.
  3. Confirmation of entry or legal stay in Paraguay.
  4. An Interpol Paraguay certificate.
  5. A completed and signed foreign investor certificate application form.
  6. A sworn statement of your commitment to open a business through SUACE and invest in Paraguay — notarized.
  7. An investment project profile, signed by the applicant.

Minimum investment amounts by track

From 70,000 USD — the production track (with a business plan and 5 formal jobs created); from 200,000 USD — real estate or financial instruments; from 150,000 USD — tourism (with a business plan and semi-annual reporting). The official document listing the CIE documents doesn't split the list by track — it appears the same package is filed regardless of the chosen regime, and the higher thresholds are governed by the text of Resolución MIC N° 0283/2026 itself, not by the certificate's document list.

What isn't specifiedThe SUACE document doesn't specify whether the "real estate" and "financial instruments" tracks require additional documents (e.g., a purchase agreement or deposit confirmation) beyond the basic list above — we clarify this individually during a consultation.

The CIE certificate is officially processed within 5 business days of submitting the complete document package. Permanent residency itself, per industry estimates, takes another roughly 3–6 months on top — this isn't an official rule, just an estimate.

Sources: the official suace.gov.py PDF (documents for the CIE, primary source); IMI Daily, Fragomen (thresholds by track and residency timelines). Confidence on the document list — high; on the timeline for obtaining residency itself — medium (an industry estimate, not an official rule).

The general conversion to permanent residency without investment is described on the "Permanent Residency" page. If you're considering the investor route — request a consultation and we'll work out which track fits you.
