Ongoing Compliance

Ongoing compliance is a subscription service that keeps your resident status valid all the way from temporary to permanent, so it isn't lost over a missed filing.

This is a continuation of "Residency", not an alternative to it: residency opens the status, and compliance keeps it valid for as long as it runs — usually 2 to 5 years before converting to Permanente.

What triggers this obligation

After getting Residencia Temporal, a foreigner must register a RUC (a unique taxpayer number tied to the Cédula) and file tax returns regularly — usually monthly — including zero filings when there was no activity.

What this looks like in practice

An illustrative diagram of the cycle that repeats every month while your resident status is active.

What the subscription covers

  • Bookkeeping — including zero filings when there was no activity in the period.
  • Deadline tracking — so no filing gets missed.
  • Case status monitoring all the way from Temporal to Permanente, which usually takes 2 to 5 years.

Why this is a separate service, not a bonus to residency

Without active compliance, converting to permanent residency risks dragging on or falling through — missed filings pile up and become a problem exactly when you apply for Permanente, not before. Full subscription pricing is on the "Pricing" page.
