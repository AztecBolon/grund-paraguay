Ybycuí National Park

A park 150 km from Asunción with a waterfall and a 19th-century ironworks that now operates as a museum.

PhotoWe don't have our own photos yet — see a selection for "Ybycuí National Park La Rosada Paraguay" on Google Images →

Ybycuí is a national park in the department of Paraguarí, about 150 km southeast of Asunción, covering about 55 km². The park contains La Rosada, a 19th-century ironworks that played a key role in Paraguay's early industrial development; the plant was damaged during the Paraguayan War and now operates as an industrial heritage museum.

The park's main waterfall is Salto Guaraní, surrounded by dense vegetation, with a maintained trail. The park is noted as one of the country's most biodiverse areas — more than 200 bird species, jaguars, pumas, tapirs, capybaras — a fragment of Atlantic Forest. Hiking, swimming in natural pools, and camping are all possible; the park is closer to Asunción than most of the other natural sites on this list, making it a realistic option for a day trip.
