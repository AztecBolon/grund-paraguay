Map of Paraguay

In one place, what is otherwise scattered across several pages: where we work and where clients do their paperwork, where historical and new immigrant communities live, and where more caution is advisable.

Where we work

Our office is in Encarnación (Itapúa department): this is where clients do their paperwork. Many then stay in the Hohenau, Obligado or Bella Vista area. If you don't want to live in that area, we find another suitable place in Paraguay through other partners. How the process works overall is on the "Process & Timeline" page.

Safety by region

From our experience on the ground, Encarnación and the Itapúa department are a calmer region. In Ciudad del Este, Asunción and the Chaco, more caution is advisable. Figures and sources on safety across the country are on the "Risks & Difficulties" page.

Historical colonies: Mennonites from Russia and the USSR

  • Fernheim (Filadelfia) — founded by Mennonites who fled repression under Stalin.
  • Volendam — refugees after the Second World War (1947).
  • Neuland — also 1947, refugees from the USSR.

More on Filadelfia and the Mennonite colonies as a place to visit is on the "Filadelfia and the Mennonite colonies" page.

The new wave of immigrants

COVID and the war in Ukraine started a new wave of migration. Russians and Ukrainians now live in Colonias Unidas (Itapúa); new European immigrants are settling in Hohenau, Obligado and Bella Vista — mostly Germans, but also Russians, Ukrainians, Belgians and Poles.
