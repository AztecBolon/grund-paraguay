Corporate income tax

IRE is the tax on a company's profit from its activity in Paraguay. Paraguay has the lowest rate in the comparison: 10% vs. 21–30% elsewhere.

VAT on goods and services

VAT (IVA) is an indirect tax built into the price of most goods and services. Paraguay has one of the lowest VAT rates in the region. The US has no federal VAT — shown here is the average state sales tax.

Dividend tax

IDU is the tax on dividends paid out to a company's shareholders. 8% for Paraguayan residents — notably lower than Russia, Germany or the US.

Tax on personal-services income

IRP is the tax on personal income from services (e.g., employment or freelance work). 8–10% in Paraguay vs. progressive scales with higher top brackets elsewhere.
