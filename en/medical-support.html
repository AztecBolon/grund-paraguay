Medical support in Paraguay

Our medical support is two things: an interpreter at medical appointments, billed by the hour, and pointers on health insurance, including recommendations of typical, widely used insurers. You take out the policy directly with the insurer.

We cover Paraguay's healthcare system in detail on a separate page, "Healthcare in Paraguay" — here, in short: three levels (basic MSPBS care, the employment-linked IPS social insurance, private medicine), and for most people relocating the real question isn't "free or paid" but which private plan to choose and on what terms.

What the support includes

  • An interpreter at appointments, by the hour — we go with you to the doctor or clinic and interpret on the spot; billed hourly.
  • Insurance pointers — we explain what to look for when choosing health insurance and can recommend typical, widely used insurers. A detailed review of specific insurers' terms and handling insurance claims aren't part of the service — you sign and manage the insurance contract directly with the insurer.

Why this actually matters

Dengue is a real seasonal risk, not a rarity (more detail and sources on the "Myths & Questions" page, "Health & Climate" section). The UK's official travel portal notes directly that quality private medical care in Paraguay is mostly available in major cities, and private clinics usually require immediate on-the-spot payment — meaning insurance isn't "just in case," it's a working tool you'll likely need in your first year.

Cost

The interpreter's work is billed at an hourly rate, which we quote when you get in touch. Insurers calculate the cost of the plans themselves individually (by age and health status) and don't publish it.
