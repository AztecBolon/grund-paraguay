Costanera de Encarnación

A 27-km promenade along the Paraná River, with a beach and views of Posadas, Argentina — the hub of Encarnación's famous carnival.

PhotoWe don't have our own photos yet — see a selection for "Costanera de Encarnación Paraguay playa San José" on Google Images →

Costanera de Encarnación runs for about 27 km along the Paraná River (through Encarnación and Carmen del Paraná). The most popular, western stretch is 8.7 km, opened in 2011, with views of Posadas, Argentina, on the opposite bank. Along the promenade are Playa San José beach, a dining strip on Avenida Francia, rest areas, and several historic structures: an old grain elevator, the San José mill, and the chimney of a former factory.

The civic center on the promenade is the headquarters of Encarnación's famous carnival (corsos encarnacenos), one of the best-known carnivals in South America outside Brazil; the city's main events throughout the year are also centered here.
