Barrio San Jerónimo

Asunción's most colorful neighborhood — painted façades and a famous mural staircase with a panoramic view of the city.

PhotoWe don't have our own photos yet — see a selection for "Barrio San Jerónimo Asunción mural escalera" on Google Images →

Barrio San Jerónimo is one of Asunción's oldest and most photogenic neighborhoods: narrow streets, brightly painted houses, staircases covered in murals. The best known is Loma San Jerónimo, a mural staircase whose top offers a panoramic view of the city and the bay.

The neighborhood is small and can be covered on foot in an hour to ninety minutes; toward evening, in the soft light, it's especially popular for photography.
