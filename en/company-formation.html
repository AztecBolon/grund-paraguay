Company Formation (EAS/SRL)

Company formation is registering a legal entity in Paraguay, which lets you do business legally: issue invoices, hire staff, and open a bank account in the company's name.

We hand company registration over to a vetted partner on the ground and coordinate the process. Registration is done in person only — we don't offer an online registration "bundled with residency."

Three forms, three registration timelines

Bar chart of registration timelines: EAS via SUACE — about 72 business hours with a standard charter, S.A. — 8–30 business days, S.R.L. — 15–30 business days.

An EAS (simplified joint-stock company) is the fastest form, but with one strict condition: the legal representative must hold Paraguayan citizenship or a Paraguayan Cédula "with no exceptions." If you don't have residency yet, a proxy representative takes on this role at the start. An S.A. and an S.R.L. take longer to register, but without that requirement for the representative.

Cost

After registration: RUC and filings

A registered company follows the same logic as an individual after getting residency: it needs a RUC and regular filings to keep its status valid. This is a separate service — see "Ongoing Compliance".
