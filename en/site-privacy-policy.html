Site Privacy Policy

Draft — do not publish without legal review This document was prepared as a working draft for launching the first version of the site and has not yet been reviewed by a lawyer. Before a public launch it needs to be shown to a lawyer (ideally one practicing in Paraguay and familiar with the law applicable to the site), and the company's placeholder details need to be replaced with real ones.

This document describes how data from visitors and users of the grundparaguay.com site itself is handled — cookies, forms, analytics. It is not the same as the client confidentiality agreement, which protects the immigration-case data of clients who have already signed an agreement — both documents are needed and serve different purposes (see the explanation on the "Confidentiality" page).

1. What data we collect

1.1. Data you provide yourself: name, contact method (Telegram, email, phone), and message text — when filling out the request form on the "Contact" page.

1.2. Technical data about your site visit: anonymized web-analytics data (pages, visit duration, referral source) — provided analytics is enabled (see item 4).

1.3. Cookies — small technical files the site may store in your browser for basic site operation and analytics.

2. What the data is used for

2.1. Request-form data is used solely to contact you about a consultation and the provision of services.

2.2. Analytics data is used to understand which pages of the site are in demand and to improve the site's content.

2.3. We do not use your data for purposes unrelated to processing your request or improving the site, and we do not sell it to third parties.

4. Sharing data with third parties

4.1. We do not share data collected through the site with third parties, except for services technically necessary for the site to operate (hosting, analytics service, form-intake/CRM service) — and only to the extent needed for them to function.

5. Your rights

5.1. You can request deletion of data you submitted via the site form by writing to office@fortuna-vida.com.py or via Telegram at @grundparaguay.

6. Changes to this policy

6.1. We may update this document as the site evolves; the current version is always available at this address.

7. Contact

For questions about how the site handles data: office@fortuna-vida.com.py.
