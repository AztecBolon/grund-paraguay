Basílica de Caacupé

Paraguay's main center of Catholic pilgrimage, 50 km from Asunción — on December 8, hundreds of thousands of pilgrims gather here.

PhotoWe don't have our own photos yet — see a selection for "Basílica de Caacupé Paraguay" on Google Images →

Basílica de Nuestra Señora de Caacupé stands in the town of Caacupé, department of Cordillera, about 50 km east of Asunción. According to a 16th-century legend, a Guaraní craftsman, fleeing his pursuers, received the intercession of the Virgin Mary and carved a wooden statue of her in gratitude — the same statue is venerated in the basilica today.

Every year on December 8, the country's main religious festival takes place here — a pilgrimage honoring Our Lady of Caacupé, which by some estimates draws hundreds of thousands of people from across the country and abroad; the exact number of pilgrims should be treated as an estimate rather than official statistics — in the material gathered here, this is the only source on the topic. The building itself is a large cathedral with domes visible from a distance, the town's architectural landmark.
