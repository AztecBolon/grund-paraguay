Costanera de Asunción

The waterfront promenade along the capital's bay, opened in its present form in 2012 — views of the bay and the presidential palace, a spot for evening walks.

PhotoWe don't have our own photos yet — see a selection for "Costanera de Asunción Paraguay" on Google Images →

La Costanera de Asunción is the promenade along the bay of Asunción, rebuilt and opened in its current form in 2012. It offers views of the bay and of Palacio de los López — especially striking at sunset, when the palace is floodlit.

The promenade is a good spot for an evening walk after touring the historic center: pedestrian and bike paths, rest areas, and water views — a contrast to the dense colonial-era buildings of the center just a few minutes' walk away.
