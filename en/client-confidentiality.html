Client data confidentiality agreement

We work with people for whom relocation is a sensitive matter: some clients decide to relocate for political or financial reasons and have no interest in the details of their case surfacing anywhere. Separately, the relocation-intermediary market as a whole carries a reputational stigma due to cases of fraud — which is why it matters to us to show that we treat client data with the same seriousness as money and documents (more on how we work under a written agreement generally is on the "About Us" page).

Important — don't confuse the twoThis confidentiality agreement is not the site's footer "data processing policy" (that one governs cookies, forms, and analytics, and lives in the site footer separately — see the "Site Privacy Policy"); it is part of the main agreement we sign with every client.

What counts as protected information

Personal and passport data; the status and details of the immigration case at every stage — from the Residencia Temporal application (Residencia Temporal) to the review of a citizenship case at the Supreme Court (Corte Suprema de Justicia); financial data related to real-estate and business deals; all correspondence and documents provided to us in the course of our work.

What we commit not to do

We do not publish or share this information with third parties without the client's written consent. This rule also extends to our deal partners — lawyers, notaries (escribanos), banks — who are bound by the same confidentiality regime while working jointly on your case.

How typical client stories are used

On the "Case Studies" page we publish only illustrative scenarios — anonymized and typical, not real client cases: no names, photos, or details that would allow a case to be identified. The timelines and terms in these stories are realistic and grounded in our practice, but the story and client profile themselves are composites — not a retelling of a specific case without permission.

Data retention and deletion

Client data is retained for as long as needed to handle the case and fulfill the agreement, and can be deleted at the client's request once the work is complete — except for documents we are legally required to retain.

Where to reach us

For any questions related to this agreement or to how your data is handled, you can reach us directly via the "Contact" page.

A sample confidentiality agreement is still being prepared — see the current status on the Contact page or ask us directly; it is automatically attached to the main agreement when a service request is finalized.
