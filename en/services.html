Relocation & legalization services in Paraguay

Seven services we handle. Residency here isn't the end goal but the entry stage: the market for temporary and permanent residency processing in Paraguay is already crowded with intermediaries, and competing on price alone doesn't make sense. The real work starts after you get residency — in the ongoing support and compliance that turn a one-off transaction into a stable legal presence in the country.

Residency

Residency is a legal status that gives you the right to live and work in Paraguay. It comes in two stages: first temporary (Residencia Temporal, up to 2 years with renewal), then — usually after 2–5 years — permanent (Residencia Permanente, for 10 years with renewal); it isn't a one-off service — it's the first stage of a longer process (full timeline on the "Process & Timeline" page). We check your document package — passport, consular visa, birth certificate, marital status certificate, international criminal record certificate, and other documents under Art. 50 of Ley N° 6984/22 "De Migraciones" — before filing, not after a rejection. Since July 6, 2026, a new migration service (DNM) resolution N° 407/2026 has been in effect, with 12 applicant categories and separate requirements for each; the exact thresholds per category haven't been officially published as of this writing, so we confirm the cost and conditions for your category individually. On its own, residency doesn't settle the question of living legally in the country without ongoing support (see the next section). The full document checklist is on the "Temporary Residency" and "Permanent Residency" pages in the "Documents" section.

Investor route (SUACE / Investor Pass)

The investor route is a way to get permanent residency directly, skipping the temporary stage, by investing in the country. With investments from 70,000 USD (production track, with a business plan and 5 formal jobs created) up to 200,000 USD (real estate or financial instruments), direct access to permanent residency is possible without going through Residencia Temporal. The scheme runs under Ministry of Industry and Commerce (MIC) resolution N° 0283/2026 (as of April 21, 2026) and is administered through SUACE. The investor certificate (CIE) is officially issued within 5 business days after the full package is filed; the permanent residency itself, per industry estimates, takes another 3–6 months on top of that. This is a separate track for a wealthier segment — we don't mix it with mass-market residency. The official document list for the investor certificate is on the "Investor Route: Documents" page.
