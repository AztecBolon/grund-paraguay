Documents by Program

Different legalization routes in Paraguay require different sets of documents. Here's a checklist for each program on its own, with a source and confidence level for every point — showing where a fact is officially confirmed and where it comes only from a secondary source and is worth double-checking before filing.

How to read these checklistsThe official domains migraciones.gov.py, suace.gov.py, and bacn.gov.py were mostly unreachable for direct verification in the working environment where this material was compiled, so most of the facts below come from secondary sources (Paraguayan law firms, specialized immigration services), with a confidence note on every point. Two documents on this page were obtained directly from official sources — this is noted separately. Before treating any list below as final when filing documents, it's worth checking with a partner lawyer in Paraguay — especially wherever the confidence is marked "medium" or "low".

Temporary Residency (Residencia Temporal)

The basic package under Art. 50 of Ley N° 6984/22: passport, apostilled birth certificate and criminal record certificate, sworn translation, in-person appearance at DNM. Valid for 2 years, renewable for the same term.

Learn more →

Permanent Residency (Residencia Permanente)

Beyond the general conversion from temporary residency — a new system of 12 applicant categories (Resolución DNM N° 407/2026, in effect since July 6, 2026): each category has its own set of documents proving income or source of funds.

Learn more →

Investor Route (SUACE / Investor Pass)

Direct access to permanent residency without going through the temporary stage, from 70,000 USD in investment. Documents for the investor certificate (CIE) — per the official SUACE list.

Learn more →

Citizenship (Naturalization)

Not sooner than 3 years after getting permanent residency — the case is handled by the Supreme Court, not the migration service. The official document list — per Poder Judicial.
