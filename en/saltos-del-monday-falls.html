Saltos del Monday

A waterfall about 45 meters high near Ciudad del Este — one of the last surviving fragments of Atlantic Forest in this part of the country.

PhotoWe don't have our own photos yet — see a selection for "Saltos del Monday waterfall Paraguay" on Google Images →

Saltos del Monday is a waterfall about 45 m high and about 120 m wide on the Monday River, in the district of Presidente Franco near Ciudad del Este. The park covers 1.6 hectares plus an adjoining nature reserve of 9 hectares; it's one of the last surviving fragments of the Alto Paraná Atlantic Forest in the area of the country with the highest rainfall.

The site was historically a pre-Hispanic Guaraní settlement. It combines naturally with a visit to Itaipú and the Friendship Bridge — all three are within reach of a single trip from Ciudad del Este.
