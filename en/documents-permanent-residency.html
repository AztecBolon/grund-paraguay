Permanent Residency: Documents & the 12 Applicant Categories

After two years of temporary residency, the next step is converting to permanent residency (Residencia Permanente). Since July 6, 2026, a system of 12 applicant categories has also been in effect, with different document requirements to prove income.

Temp. residency Perm. residency Investor route Citizenship

General Conversion from Temporary Residency

The application is filed within 90 days before Residencia Temporal expires, provided the full 2-year term has been completed. Permanent residency is granted for 10 years with the option to renew, and is forfeited after a continuous absence of more than 3 years.

12 Applicant Categories — Resolución DNM N° 407/2026

The resolution (Resolución DNM N° 407/2026) specifically governs proof of economic solvency — it doesn't replace the general document package for temporary residency (see the "Temporary Residency" page), but adds category-specific requirements on top of it. It applies to cases filed since July 6, 2026.

Confidence level for the table belowThe 12-category structure is confirmed by three independent sources, but the detailed document list for each category comes from a single commercial immigration service, without a line-by-line cross-check. Before filing documents under a specific category, it's worth checking directly with DNM or a partner lawyer — especially for the "self-employed", "shareholder", and "dependent" categories, the most likely ones for the agency's clients.

General Requirements for All Categories

Documents must match the occupation declared during temporary residency; allow objective verification of real income or source of funds; and be current. Foreign documents — apostilled and translated by a sworn translator. The resolution doesn't set a single minimum income threshold for all categories — the emphasis is on the sufficiency and verifiability of the proof, not a fixed amount.

Sources: Paraguay Sovereign ("Resolution 407 Explained"), MERSAN, ADN Digital (06/25/2026). Confidence on the resolution's existence, date, and 12-category structure — high; on the documents within each category — medium.

A faster route to permanent residency — without going through the temporary stage — is described on the "Investor Route" page. The next legalization stage after permanent residency is citizenship. If you need an assessment of your category — request a consultation.
